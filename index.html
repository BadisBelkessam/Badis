Weekly Boxing Workout Plan

Monday: Technique & Footwork

  • Warm-up (10 min): Jump rope, dynamic stretches
  • Shadow Boxing (3 rounds x 3 min): Focus on jab, cross, hook, uppercut, defensive movements
  • Footwork Drills (15 min): Ladder drills, cone drills, pivot practice
  • Bag Work (3 rounds x 3 min): Light combinations, movement around the bag
  • Cool-down (5 min): Static stretches

Tuesday: Power & Conditioning

  • Warm-up (10 min): Jogging, arm circles, leg swings
  • Heavy Bag (5 rounds x 3 min): Power shots, continuous combinations
  • Circuit Training (3 rounds):
    • Burpees (1 min)
    • Kettlebell Swings (1 min)
    • Medicine Ball Slams (1 min)
    • Rest (1 min)
  • Core Work (10 min): Planks, Russian twists, leg raises
  • Cool-down (5 min): Static stretches

Wednesday: Active Recovery / Light Cardio

  • Light Jog or Cycling (30-45 min)
  • Stretching & Mobility (20 min): Focus on tight areas
  • Foam Rolling (10 min)

Thursday: Sparring Drills & Defense

  • Warm-up (10 min): Jump rope, dynamic stretches
  • Partner Drills (20 min): Slip and roll drills, parrying, counter-punching
  • Light Sparring (3-5 rounds x 3 min): Focus on defense and controlled offense (if applicable and with proper supervision)
  • Double-End Bag / Reflex Bag (15 min): Improve timing and reflexes
  • Cool-down (5 min): Static stretches

Friday: Endurance & Speed

  • Warm-up (10 min): Jump rope, dynamic stretches
  • Speed Bag (15 min): Improve hand-eye coordination and shoulder endurance
  • Pad Work (4 rounds x 3 min): Fast combinations, high volume (with a coach/partner)
  • Interval Training (20 min):
    • Sprint (30 sec)
    • Walk (90 sec)
  • Cool-down (5 min): Static stretches

Saturday: Strength & Cross-Training

  • Warm-up (10 min): Light cardio, dynamic stretches
  • Strength Training (45 min): Compound movements (squats, deadlifts, push-ups, rows)
  • Swimming or Hiking (30-60 min): Alternative cardio
  • Cool-down (5 min): Static stretches

Sunday: Rest & Recovery

  • Complete rest or light active recovery like walking.
  • Focus on nutrition and hydration.
